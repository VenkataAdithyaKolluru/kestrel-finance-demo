## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-velocity financial orchestration and agentic intelligence. Combining the mathematical rigor of Stripe, the corporate spend velocity of Ramp, and the focused craft of Linear, the visual narrative is precise, mission-critical, and relentlessly sophisticated.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Technical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Depth**:
- **Monolithic Clarity:** Information is parsed at executive speed with zero extraneous noise. Surfaces are layered with sub-pixel borders, fine glassmorphism backdrops, and deliberate tonal zoning.
- **Engineered Restraint:** Backgrounds remain dark, calm, and grounded in deep obsidian and slate navy tones, allowing live telemetry and autonomous operations to hold absolute focus.
- **Luminescent Telemetry:** Ambient micro-glows and pinpoint state indicators signal system velocity—electric indigo drives AI orchestration, emerald confirms immutable financial settlements, and warm amber highlights variance audits.
- **Tactile Density:** Compact typography paired with tactile, low-radius containers creates a cockpit-like interface built for finance engineers, CFOs, and autonomous treasury agents.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated luminescence over deep slate-navy surfaces, optimized for high-density legibility and rapid anomaly recognition.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Reserved for agentic execution, generative synthesis, automated routing states, and primary interactive commitments. It signals cognitive compute and forward progression.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Synthetic Emerald):** High-saturation validation for verified ledger lines, settled transactions, liquidity surges, and cryptographic security checks.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Radiant):** Dedicated to variance warnings, pending multi-sig approvals, compliance thresholds, and rate-limit throttles.
- **Neutral (`#090D16` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** The root substrate. A cool, blue-tinted void that rejects pure lifeless black in favor of deep atmospheric depth.

### Surface Tiers & Overlays
- **Canvas Base:** `#090D16`
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards/Workspaces):** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` layered with subtle backdrop blur.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Modals/Overlays):** `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)` layered with higher opacity.
- **Sub-pixel Borders:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on default containers, elevating to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)` when driven by active AI focus states.
- **Text & Contrast:** Pure white (`#F8FAFC`) for primary numeric metrics, cold slate (`#94A3B8`) for metadata, and muted zinc (`#475569`) for inactive anchors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scanning, tabular alignment, and zero ambiguity in quantitative representation.

- **Primary Typeface (Inter):** Leveraged for all display, headline, and core narrative body copy. Configured with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke the dense, razor-sharp discipline of high-performance product interfaces.
- **Monospaced Engine (JetBrains Mono):** Mandated for all transaction hashes, currency amounts, latency metrics, audit trail logs, and micro-labels. Provides rock-solid columnar alignment across fast-updating real-time data feeds.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large metrics and KPIs use `display` weight with tabular figures (`tnum`). Section titles never exceed `headline-md` within embedded dashboards to maintain layout efficiency and component density.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **Fluid Composite Grid** structured around rapid operational density and responsive paneling:

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column adaptive fluid grid flanked by a collapsible 240px utility sidebar. Default gutters are fixed at `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem / 24px) with structural canvas margins at `margin-desktop` (2rem / 32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Sidebars collapse into icon rails; metrics blocks reflow to dual-column cards; gutters step down to `gutter` (1rem / 16px).
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column flow. Complex ledger tables convert to nested virtualized cards. Padding snaps tightly to `margin` (1rem / 16px) to maximize screen area for mission-critical telemetry.
- **Rhythm & Gaps:** Component internal layouts rely strictly on `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for related inline items, `space-md` (12px) for input padding and item rows, and `space-lg` (20px) to delineate isolated metric clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined synthesis of **Tonal Glassmorphism** and **Sub-pixel Boundary Outlines** rather than heavy muddy drop-shadows.

### Structural Stratification
1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** Pure `#090D16` void with an optional faint, wide-radius radial gradient (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.05)` at 900px blur) centered beneath primary visualization widgets.
2. **Level 1 (Data Cards, Sidebars, Grids):** Background fill of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a precise `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` perimeter.
3. **Level 2 (Popovers, Filter Drawers, Context Menus):** Background fill of `rgba(24, 33, 58, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, supported by a subtle directional shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
4. **Level 3 (Command Palettes, Critical Modals):** Background fill of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95)`, illuminated by a hairline top border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` transitioning to side borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Reactive Light Accents
- **Autonomous Activity:** Elements running agentic compute display an animated edge gradient: linear transition between `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)` and `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0)`.
- **Validation Ring:** Verified entities cast a localized ambient shadow: `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (1)** geometry: an intentional choice balancing modern enterprise precision with tactile digital craft.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to micro-components: status badges, inline code tags, checkboxes, scrollbars, and table row selections.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** The foundational container curve. Used for all primary cards, text input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, and navigation nodes. Keeps dense UI layouts structured without wasting space.
- **Structural Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Applied exclusively to floating viewports, outer workspace modals, slide-out configuration sheets, and command overlays.
- **Pill Exception:** Interactive chip filters, AI status toggles, and verified avatar badges may use full circular clipping (`9999px`) to immediately denote non-structural, interactive status tokens.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary (Action):** Background of `var(--primary_color_hex)`, high-contrast white text, zero shadow in resting state. On hover: subtle scale transition accompanied by a soft backlight `0 0 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary (Subtle Glass):** Background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text color `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` surface fill and border opacity increases to `0.18`.
- **Agentic / AI Mode:** Radial gradient fill combining deep violet to indigo, framed by an animated running border reflecting background sync status.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 36px (compact density). Deep slate background `rgba(10, 15, 28, 0.6)`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, font family `Inter` for regular queries, switching automatically to `JetBrains Mono` for currency and equation inputs. Focus ring: `0 0 0 1px #6366F1`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 14px boxes with 3px border radius. Border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked state transitions seamlessly to `#6366F1` with an inner SVG checkmark drawn in sharp white stroke.

### Data Displays & Cards
- **Financial Metric Cards:** Frosted slate backdrop, 8px radius, separated into an upper label row (monospaced tracking, 11px uppercase) and a primary monetary metric (`display` size, tabular numerals). Variance pill anchored at bottom right: emerald green (`#10B981`) text inside `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` pill.
- **Ledger Tables:** Ultra-dense rows (36px desktop height). Subtle bottom stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. Alternating hover surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`. Timestamp and balance columns right-aligned in monospaced font.

### Status Indicators & Chips
- **Verified State:** Micro-dot (6px) with an emerald pulse ring paired with `label-sm` font.
- **Audit Anomaly:** Warm amber outline chip with inline warning glyph, non-dismissible until reconciled.
- **Agentic Activity Chip:** Electric indigo pill showing a micro-pulse radar dot, signifying background calculation or autonomous execution in flight.
